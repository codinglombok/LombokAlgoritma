# Code of Conduct — LombokAlgoritma

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and inclusive
- Focus on the algorithm and code, not the person
- Accept constructive criticism graciously
- Support newcomers and learners

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Publishing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to: conduct@codinglombok.dev

Maintainers will review and may take action including warnings, temporary bans, or permanent bans.
